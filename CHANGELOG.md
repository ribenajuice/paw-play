## Unreleased

Not in a build yet. These changes are on the Paw Pour, Paw Kitchen and Paw Trace branches, waiting for review and a test on a real phone.

- There is a new game on the home screen: **Paw Pour**. Tap a tube of coloured liquid to lift it, then tap another tube to pour. Sort the colours until each one fills its own tube.
- Every Paw Pour round can be finished. The game checks before it deals.
- If your child pours themselves into a dead end, the game gently undoes the last pour after a moment. There is no message and no "you lost".
- A wrong tap makes the tube wobble a little. Nothing else happens.
- Each colour has its own shape as well, so children who can't tell the colours apart can still play.
- Each round is a little bigger than the last, up to 9 tubes. It starts again from the small round when you leave for the home screen.
- Paw Pour has no sound yet. Paw Match has none either.
- There is another new game on the home screen: **Paw Kitchen**. An animal customer asks for a burger, a pizza or an ice cream as a row of pictures. Tap ingredients to build the dish. Tap one again, or its faint spot in the tray, to take it off.
- The serve button sleeps until the dish is exactly what was asked for. Extras never cause a scolding, they just keep it asleep. When it wakes, tap it and the customer cheers with sparkles and hearts, then the next customer walks up.
- Orders start with 2 ingredients and grow to 5. Each ingredient has its own shape, so children who can't tell colours apart can still play.
- Paw Kitchen has no sound yet, like the other games. Leaving for the home screen starts it again from the first customer.
- Fixed: on Paw Match's "you did it!" screen, the door on the home button's little house was invisible. You can now see it.
- There is a fourth new game on the home screen: **Paw Trace**. A wide, soft path shows what to trace and a friendly frog waits where to start. Drag a finger along the path and it fills with purple. Colour only appears where the finger is on the path. Off the path nothing happens, and lifting your finger and touching down again anywhere on the path carries on.
- When a line is nearly covered it finishes itself with a little sparkle, and the frog hops to the next line. If your child stops for a few seconds, the frog walks along the line to show the way.
- Letters and numbers with more than one line, like E, 4 and A, can be traced in any order and in either direction.
- When a shape is finished there is a short celebration and one big green paw button. Tapping it shows the next one. Mashing the button only moves on once.
- It starts with lines and curves, then shapes, then the numbers 1 to 9 and 0, then the capital letters, and then keeps going with a random mix of all 46. Every third one is an easier one from before. Nobody picks a level.
- Only the first finger paints, so a second finger or a resting palm can't smear paint around.
- Paw Trace has no sound yet, like the other games. Leaving for the home screen starts it again from the first line.
